Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Attendance Manager handles information when you use the Attendance Manager desktop application and its optional Google integrations.

1. Information stored by the application

Attendance Manager may store information required to operate the application, including student records, course and section information, class information, enrollment information, attendance records, and application settings.

The application's core data is designed to be stored locally on the user's computer.

2. Google account information

If you choose to connect a Google account, the application may receive basic account information made available through Google's OAuth authorization process, such as your Google account identifier, name, and email address.

This information is used to identify the connected Google account and provide Google features explicitly enabled by the user.

3. Google API data

Depending on the features enabled by the user, Attendance Manager may access Google Drive, Google Sheets, or Google Forms through the permissions displayed on Google's consent screen.

Attendance Manager does not request access to Google data unless that access is necessary for a supported feature. The application does not sell Google user data.

4. How Google data is used

Google data is used only to provide Google functionality selected by the user, including supported operations such as:

  • connecting and identifying the user's Google account;
  • working with application-related Google Drive files;
  • working with supported Google Sheets features;
  • working with supported Google Forms features.

5. Data sharing

Attendance Manager does not sell personal information or Google user data.

Information is not shared with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

6. Storage and security

Local application data and authentication information are stored on the user's computer.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on their device, including operating-system access controls and protection against unauthorized access.

7. Disconnecting Google

Users can disconnect their Google account from Attendance Manager using the application's Google account settings when that feature is available.

Users may also revoke the application's Google permissions through their Google Account security settings.

8. Children's privacy

Attendance Manager is an administrative software tool intended for educators, administrators, or other authorized users. It is not intended to collect information directly from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
